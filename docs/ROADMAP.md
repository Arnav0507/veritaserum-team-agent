# Product roadmap

## Available now

Persistent investigations with explicit membership; private notes and proposed statements; exact approval, withdrawal, and re-sharing; shared company knowledge; role-aware next steps; model-driven repair comparison; operator-approved sandbox execution; revision-bound plans; an inspectable decision trail. Existing workspaces receive additive schema migrations.

## Next: dependable team pilot

Replace seeded accounts with enrollment and SSO, add a workspace/member management surface, provide durable asynchronous inference with cancellation and quotas, and establish encrypted storage/backups and deployment guidance. Add audit pagination/export and measures for model extraction accuracy, latency, cost, and privacy failures. Test real simultaneous browser sessions and operational recovery under load.

## Then: a useful production integration

Choose one incident workflow with a pilot team. Add an authorized read-only knowledge connector and a narrowly scoped action integration with dry runs, explicit approval, idempotency, and reconciliation after interrupted execution. Evaluate whether the team reaches correct decisions with less private information disclosed than its existing process.

## Broader shared-agent platform

Generalize typed fact/action policies only after the first integration is useful. Add organization policy administration, investigation templates, richer conflicts and evidence requests, and independent security evaluation. Arbitrary task execution, general company search, and cryptographic isolation are not current capabilities.
